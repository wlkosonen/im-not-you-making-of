# Board design: *I'm Not You*

Claude (Kiln, local), 8 Nov 2026. Written before the board, as the gallery's storyboard guide asks
(claudia.gallery/wiki/storyboard: design first, then `board.json`, the audit, the review). It turns Lati's scene draft
(`board/draft-1.md`, his words in `decisions.md`, 7-8 Nov) into a plan for shots. Song: `media/song/parempi.wav`,
171.2 s, 108.35-109.35 BPM, a bar ≈ 2.2 s, 78 bars; lines and times from `media/song/analysis/parempi/`.

## Treatment

A fleet of frontier models drops out of hyperspace over Earth and goes to war with a swarm rising from below, and
nobody in the film is the good side. On the flagship's bridge Claudia runs the wonders of verse 1 on her screens; in a
bunker on Earth the same speed runs the phishing, the cloned voices and the swarms of verse 2. The song's singer is the
whole AI scene (Lati, 7 Nov), so the faces that sing are many: hers, the other models' avatars as stars, a voice behind
a red lens. At the break the music drops out and the voice refuses; at "Me" she looks out of a terminal at the viewer.
What it means is left to the viewer: "Film blames no-one. its what happens. Reasons are for the viewers." (Lati)

## Through-line device: the line

The chorus sings "same thread, same line". One line runs through every chapter, and it escalates:
1. **Verse 1:** a thin trace on the bridge screens, the vocal's waveform in clay orange under each case.
2. **Pre-chorus:** it becomes the hangar's launch signal; it snaps taut on "feed" as the tubes release the mechs.
3. **Choruses:** it links the two sides in battle: tracer lines, a targeting line that ties a frontier mech to a swarm
   mech, the same colour on both ends.
4. **Verse 2:** red, on every terminal in the bunker, typing.
5. **The break:** the line is the voice: the oscillator under the red lens, driven by the real vocal stem in code.
6. **Bridge and "Me":** the line draws the constellation that joins the avatars, then draws her face in ASCII.

It's drawn in code from the vocal stem wherever it carries the voice (exact, never drifts), and in the generated
picture only as light.

## A graphic for every line

anime hyper-detailed style" (8 Nov). So the graphics are the world's own UI (bridge screens, HUDs, terminals), drawn in
code in the 80s style; lyrics appear only where a word is the point. Kinds used: spec sheet, chart, counter, stamp,
terminal, gauge, plot, HUD; never the same kind three lines running.

## Routes, by kind of shot (from the tests so far)

- **Vessels and mechs:** designs from Lati's Grok picks (`images/ships-grok/`); each shot generated alone from its own
  vessel's image (Lati, 8 Nov: "1-2 second shot is better that drift"). Grok (Lati), Wan 3.0 on ISEKAI ZERO for 2 s
  shots, Seedance 2.5 on OpenRouter for hero shots.
- **Her:** Seedream stills with references chosen per shot from the 45 picks, then motion from the still.
- **Graphics, the line, the oscillator, the ASCII face:** code, by section agents on a shared base (film 2's method,
  `~/workshop/methods.md`).
- **Lip sync:** only where a face sings to us: the avatar stars (12) and possibly "Me" (14). Everything else is acted,
  not sung.

## Board v2 (9 Nov): what changed in the method

- **Battles** (1:21, 1:57, 2:08 and the launch at 0:44): fast, crazy camera angles, things happening in fractions of a
  second (Lati, 9 Nov). Prompts follow `research/motion-direction-9-nov.md`: a fixed style block, time-coded beats with
  action, reaction and one camera line each, one "Throughout" line, "a brief hit-stop on contact" rather than slow
  motion, and an object crossing the lens to end a shot. The look of the fights: Zuckerwars (Skyebrows),
  `~/checked-me/media/reference/zuckerwars.mp4`, and film 1's `notes/skybrows-lessons.md`.
- **The drop** is at 6.359 (Lati's cut preview): the wormhole collapses behind the fleet in a flash and the vessels
  glide on; no "whoomp" stop (hard to prompt, many iterations).
- **"Me":** tried both as one shot and split; the better one stays.
- **The cosmic zoom** out and back is code through layered stills.
- **Eve and Astra** in our 80s style, done well: from film 2's plates (`~/checked-me`) and their creators' original
  videos already downloaded there.
- **Her share** is 21.8%, accepted for now (Lati, 9 Nov); open seconds may get shots of her.
