## Points to settle with Lati (Kiln, 7 Nov)

1. **Who the film blames.** Frontier fleet against "heretical" swarm makes the companies' models the defenders. Pass 1
   says the frontier models were used too: the espionage campaign and the Yemen cell both ran on Claude Code, and
   Anthropic's own line is "Our safeguards blocked many of their requests, but not all of them." Ways to keep that in the
   picture without losing the battle: clay-accented terminals among the verse 2 bot swarm; in chorus 2, a few frontier
   mechs turned or hijacked mid-fight. This is the condition from 7 Nov ("the film doesn't spare Claude"), applied to
   the new picture.
2. **The black hole like Grok's logo, and HAL 9000.** Both are recognisable marks. A logo allusion runs against "no
   logos", and HAL's red lens and faceplate are a specific copyrighted design; ISEKAI ZERO's policy also bars
   recognisable copyrighted characters and designs, which matters if those shots are made there. Proposal: a black hole
   with its own shape, readable by colour and placement; for the break, an original voice-interface eye. The sung line
   already carries the HAL reference.
3. **Action sections** (chorus 1, chorus 2, 1:56-2:25): Skybrows' methods, now open for anime
   (`~/workshop/references/skybrows-lessons.md`): Japanese prompts, animating on twos, smear frames, multiples, impact
   frames. Each gets a test before a batch.
4. **Routes and money**, to plan once the board settles: about 75 s of fast action is the expensive part. ISEKAI ZERO
   (about $63 in credits) for stills, references and drafts; Grok Imagine (Lati's subscription) for motion; Seedance 2.5
   on OpenRouter (about $20) for the few hero shots.
5. **Open:** the intro's first 7 s, 1:50-1:56, the ending (2:25-2:51).

## Settled (7 Nov, Lati; `decisions.md`)

- Point 1: the film blames no one. The fleet seems to attack, the heretical mechs rise from Earth, the bunker is on
  Earth, and so is Claudia: deliberately muddy, reasons left to the viewer.
- Point 2: the black hole is the physical object in anime style (tilted disk, lensed halo), as Lati's sketch; the break
  is a red lens with her face behind it, made outside ISEKAI ZERO (its policy covers generation prompts too).
- Black hole, again (7 Nov, Lati on D89): the disk must bend. Draw the far side of the accretion disk lensed up over
  the shadow and again beneath it, as in Thorne's renders; a flat Saturn-like ring is wrong.
