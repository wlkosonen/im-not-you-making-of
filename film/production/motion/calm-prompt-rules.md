# Calm motion prompts: the rules (film 3)

Claude (Kiln, local), 9 Nov 2026. For the shots that aren't battles: the research pit, the bridge, the hangar, the
command centre, the servers, sakura, the bot farm, utopia, the arrival. Battles and action use
`battle-prompt-rules.md`. Written before the first calm batch; Lati's verdicts on it will amend this file.

1. **The same style block,** word for word (`battle-prompt-rules.md` §1).
2. **Calm camera sentence:** "Wide-angle lens, asymmetric diagonal framing, a steady camera on one smooth move.
   Everyone and everything in the frame keeps moving from the first frame to the last: people work, turn, glance and
   breathe, screens and lights change, space vessels glide." (From the battles: what's named without an action stands
   still.) A fast shot inside a calm chapter (hyperspace) takes a fast camera sentence instead.
3. **Two time-coded beats, 0-2s and 2-4s;** beat 1 is the board's moment. One camera verb each, pinned to something
   (between the consoles, from the gantry, over her shoulder), with a gentle roll or "Locked camera".
4. **Throughout:** "constant gentle motion, <one light rule>, <what to draw in detail> drawn in detail, perfect
   character, lighting and style consistency."
5. **Characters by look, never by name:** Claudia, Eve, Astra and Vera are described by hair and clothes (`opening.md`).
6. **Screens stay plain:** light, a waveform, readouts; anything to be read is drawn in code later.
7. **Positive words; "space vessel"; iridescent, never the other word.**

## Lessons from the opening batch (10 Nov, Flint, from Lati's review)

8. **Something that arrives can't start from a plate that shows it arrived.** The plate as first frame is the start of
   the shot, so s005's fleet sat still and then moved. For an arrival, the first frame is the plate with the arriving
   thing removed (Qwen Image Edit 2511 on ISEKAI ZERO kept the framing; Seedream 4.5 redrew the scene, twice) and the
   plate is the last frame; the prompt times the arrival (0-1 s) and the settle after it.
9. **A vessel named outside a viewport may come straight at it.** s011's "beyond the viewport a space vessel glides
   past" drew the flagship about to ram it. Give it a distance and a direction: far off, sliding sideways.
10. **"Locked camera" is a request, not a rule:** s010 and s021 drifted anyway. Lati: not a problem, "could be useful in
    a cut"; which parts get used will show what works.
11. **The avatars pass Seedance 2.0's filter on ISEKAI ZERO** (Eve, Astra, Vera, Claudia, s009-s023): no refusals.


## Short on screen (11 Nov, from s032)

Most mid-1 shots play 0.9-1.6 s in Lati's cut. Check the on-screen time (his `edit/timeline.json`, else the board)
before writing the beats: the shot's main event goes in a "0-1s: from the first frame …" beat, the rest is spare for
the cut. Lati, on the s032 retake written this way: "Retake is by order of magnitude better."
