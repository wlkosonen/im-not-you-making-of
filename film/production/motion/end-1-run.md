# The end: the nuclear bursts, two looks (11 Nov 2026)

Claude (Kiln, local). Lati, 11 Nov: "for nuclear burst we need to do 2 plates, and gen them into motion with two
options, anime fireball look that fades into disc with flashes and more realistic one, prompt up to you, since you
know better what is realistic in this matter".

The board puts the bursts in orbit along the equator, where the battle is (s108 "destroying every combatant"). In
vacuum a nuclear burst makes no fireball and no mushroom cloud: a flash brighter than the sun, a short-lived expanding
shell of glowing debris, and, in the upper atmosphere beneath, an artificial aurora of red and green spreading along
the magnetic field (Starfish Prime, 1962, 400 km over the Pacific, was seen this way from Hawaii). That is the
realistic option; the anime option keeps the fireballs and fades them into glowing discs with flashes.

cel fireballs along the equator), `s108-2-*` (real: white points with pale shells over the equator, red and green
aurora on the night side). Motion: Seedance 2.5, 6 s each ($1.39), one take covering s108 to s110 (bursts, fade,
the empty orbit). Clip book `end-1.json`.
