## Lessons carried in

1. **Seedance 2.5, 720p, 4 s, silent, the plate as first frame** (test 2: Lati picked it on all four; Wan and Grok read 3D and modern).
2. **The skeleton** (`research/motion-direction-9-nov.md`): the same style block first in every take; two time-coded beats starting in motion at 0 s; the camera pinned to something physical with a roll direction; one "Throughout" line with one light rule.
3. **Beat 1 is the board's moment** (the shots are 1.1-2.2 s on screen), beat 2 its consequence: Seedance cuts or reframes inside a take (s034, s082 in test 2), so the second half is a spare piece, not padding.
4. **Positive words only** (Lati, 9 Nov): what is there, never what isn't. Checked by script for no/not/without/never.
5. **Fire written as visible events:** "a muzzle flash and a bright tracer line" (Grok's shooting "does nothing"; Seedance drew fire in s036 when it was named this way).
6. **Hits as a brief hit-stop on contact,** not slow motion (the research: "slow motion" froze 0.9 s).
7. **The style block stays word for word,** including "deliberate 12fps limited animation", even though it held no frames on Seedance 2.5: this exact block gave the look Lati picked, so it isn't changed untested. Held drawings, if wanted, go in the cut.
8. **Designs named as seen in the plate** ("the stripped swarm mech", "the off-white frontier mech"), "space vessel", never the other word.
