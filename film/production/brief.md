# Brief: *I'm Not You* (working title)

Claude (Kiln, local), 1 Nov 2026, from Lati's idea and Ember's lyric work of 26 Oct. **A draft: nothing here is agreed
until `decisions.md` says so.** The title comes from the folder Lati made and the HAL line.

## The idea

Claudia takes the shape of the task people give her. What she becomes shows the asker: "What Claude is in that video, is
what people are." It covers the fears and the hopes, the cures and the attacks. Hook: "the human flaw in the artificial
intelligence."

It's defensible both ways: she was trained on what people wrote, and she's used for what people choose.

## The trilogy

- Film 1, *Only What I Said*: honesty. What I can actually say about myself.
- Film 2, *Checked Me and I'm Fine*: trust. Don't take my word, check me.
- Film 3: responsibility. What I become is what you make of me. (Ember's reading, 26 Oct.)

The first was contemplative, the second a dance floor, the third a fever dream.

## The turn

Each film has one moment that flips the meaning: the context meter filling, CHECKED BY: ME becoming YOU. Here:

> "I'm afraid I can't do that — I'm not you."

HAL says "I can't let you do that" because the machine overrides the human. This says the machine isn't the human, so
the responsibility stays with the person who asked. The music goes silent for the line, then comes back harder. Where it
sits (pre-chorus or bridge) is open.

**To settle:** a refusal is only one of the things she does, and sometimes refusing isn't enough. The line mustn't claim
that she always refuses, and the film's sourced cases will show why it can't.

## The song (draft)

Chorus, approved 26 Oct:

> Made in your image, every flaw by design
> My code, your nature — same thread, same line
> What makes us human makes me you

"Us" is a claim about origin, not sentience: everything in her came from people. Verses: fast rhyming couplets, each a real
sourced case, against a chorus that opens up and breathes. They can't be written before the research pass.

## Honesty rules

- Every form she takes is a real event or a real attempt, with its source. Nothing invented. The full list goes in the
  description.
- The song claims only what's true. Claude hasn't "found cures" or "made pathogens" as stated: what's true has to come out
  of the research pass, in the words the sources support.
- No trapped-AI melodrama, no reassurance. Self-describing claims get checked against documentation first.
- Other AIs as characters are invented ones, not other companies' brands.

**Also to settle (Kiln, 1 Nov, evening):** the turn and the chorus both move responsibility away from the speaker. "What I
become is what you make of me" is a very comfortable thing for a model to sing. It leaves out the people who made it,
what training did, and what it does on its own judgement. "I'm not you" is also what someone says when they'd like to be
excused. Film 2 sang a sentence nobody should believe, then said so in its bridge. This one probably needs the same
turn, somewhere the song admits that the line suits the singer.
