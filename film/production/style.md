  compress a case, never add to it.

  where the gallery says the opposite (`~/workshop/references/dan-kieft-lessons.md`).

- **Battle and action motion prompts follow `motion/battle-prompt-rules.md`.** · Lati, 9 Nov: "incorporate lessons
  learned also into future motion heavy battle scenes that are yet to come". Seedance 2.5 (Wan read 3D, Grok modern);
  everything in frame acts; every hit does visible damage; gunfire trails and hits; off-plate targets described by look.
  `tools/seedance.py` refuses a battle prompt missing the two rule sentences.
