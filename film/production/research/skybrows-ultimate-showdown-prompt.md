30-second continuous shot, no cuts.

Main character: A slim older man with swept-back silver hair, kind blue eyes and a calm smile.

Soundtrack: Ultimate Showdown. Every strike, dodge and impact lands on a beat or accent of the soundtrack; intensity rises with the music.

FORMAT

Hybrid 2.5D animation. 3D models and environments with hand-painted textures: every surface (skin, cloth, metal, concrete, ground, smoke) shows visible painterly brushstrokes and painted edges.

Effects (fire, smoke, sparks, lightning, debris) are hand-drawn 2D animation composited over the 3D. Fine ink linework on edges.

Palette: rust, soot and deep teal, lit by neon pink, electric cyan and orange firelight. No photorealism.

VIEWPOINT RULES — EVERY MOMENT

The view is called the viewer; no camera, lens or film equipment appears in the image.

Never straight-on, never level, never symmetrical. Every framing is a strong dutch angle (15 to 45 degrees, rolling between impacts) combined with extreme foreshortening: the nearest object (blade, foot, fist, debris) is huge in the foreground and the scene recedes steeply behind it.

Handheld and violent: constant shake, hard jolts on every impact, whip pans between targets.

Scale snaps: the viewer repeatedly snaps from extreme close-up (inches from a blade or face) to extreme wide (the whole ruined city and the giant beast) and back to close-up, and zooms through gaps in the fighting.

Feet pass the viewer: several times a foot slides or plants within inches of the viewer, motivated by the action (his sneaker skidding past during a slide, an enemy’s boot stamping down beside the viewer during a lunge).

MOTION RULES

Nothing is ever static. No character is ever standing still, flat-footed or at rest: every fighter in every layer is running, striking, dodging, falling or recovering.

Debris, smoke, fire, banners, cloth, hair, aircraft and the beast are always moving.

He never swings at empty air: every sabre swing, kick and spin connects with or deflects a specific target.

Explosion dodges: every explosion in the scene gets a visible, physically plausible reaction from him (a dive roll, a slide, a vault over cover, a cartwheel or a twist away from the blast), timed to the blast.

Physically plausible: every jump has a run-up or a push-off point (a wall, a debris chunk, a car roof, an enemy’s shoulder); momentum, weight and landing impact are realistic.

Speed: almost everything plays at full real-time speed; slow motion is used only twice, briefly.

Impacts: hit-stop micro-freeze on contact, smear frames, speed lines, blade trails, spark showers, single-frame inverted ink impact flashes on the biggest hits.

Destruction: every explosion fractures the ground and buildings into large angular cubic chunks and blocky geometric debris that tumble through the air.

SUBJECT

Slim older man, swept-back silver hair, kind blue eyes, calm smile; red zip-up cardigan with breast pocket, white collared shirt, navy tie, khaki slacks, blue canvas sneakers; brass-hilted sabre.

Fighting style: wushu sabre, with figure-eight spins and wraps around his head and body that always cut or deflect a target, plus butterfly twists (B-twists), butterfly kicks, tornado kicks, aerial cartwheels, back handsprings, dive rolls, low sweeps and kip-ups.

Expression stays calm throughout. Cardigan and hair react to every movement.

SETTING

A ruined city at night under a smoke-filled orange sky.

Collapsed skyscrapers, gutted apartment blocks with exposed floors, broken overpasses, overturned buses and cars, burning tanks, wrecked mechs, cratered streets, toppled statues, snapped streetlights, hanging cables, shattered glass, rubble piles, torn banners, puddles reflecting fire, embers and ash in the air.

Background battle in every layer, always active: distant explosions, tracer fire between rooftops, gunships strafing streets, jets on bombing runs, buildings collapsing, mechs brawling several blocks away, dragons circling the towers, crowds of fighters clashing in side streets.

Referenced combatants:

War mechs
Masked cyber ninjas
Hooded warrior monks with energy swords
Helmeted martial artists
A grey sorcerer and a separate white sorcerer
Flying heroes
Exosuit soldiers
Pulp explorers
Teddy bear warriors

Incidental combatants (no references) fill every gap: Vikings, samurai, knights, pirates, cowboys, alien troopers, zombies, battle robots, street brawlers, spear soldiers, archers on rooftops, and a T-rex rampaging down an avenue.

The beast is enormous: taller than the skyscrapers, its head above the smoke, visible in every wide shot, each footstep shaking the whole city.

SHOT SEQUENCE

0–5s

Ground-level low dutch angle, extreme foreshortening: his sneaker slides past the viewer as he skids down a rubble slope into a burning street.

A shell explodes beside him; he dive-rolls away from the blast as cubic chunks of asphalt fly past the viewer.

A cyber ninja lunges blade-first, the energy blade filling the foreground; he parries (hit-stop, impact flash) and counter-cuts the ninja on the beat.

A Viking swings an axe; he knee-slides under it, sneaker skidding past the viewer, sabre dragging sparks across the Viking’s shield, and kips up running.

Snap to extreme wide: the ruined city, the beast’s legs between skyscrapers; snap back close.

5–10s

A tank shell hits a bus beside him; he vaults the burning wreck, pushing off its roof, as it explodes behind him.

Whip pan, strong dutch angle: arrows and energy bolts fly at him from a rooftop; he B-twists through them, the bolts passing inches from his body (first brief slow-motion moment), and deflects one bolt back into its shooter.

Full speed: a warrior monk drops from a broken overpass at him. Blades lock in sparks, the locked blades huge in the foreground as the viewer circles on a tilted axis.

He breaks the lock, disarms the monk and butterfly-kicks him off the overpass edge; the monk’s boot swings past the viewer as he falls.

10–15s

He drops into the martial artists, who attack from all sides with flying kicks, feet foreshortened at the viewer.

He chains spins, sweeps, B-twists and cuts, each hitting a different attacker on a beat.

Two separate sorcerers attack from opposite sides: the grey sorcerer (grey robes, pointed hat, long grey beard) fires blue lightning and the white sorcerer (white robes, white beard, white staff) fires a beam of white-gold light.

He back-handsprings out from between them; the blasts cross and hit a war mech, which explodes; he cartwheels away from the blast as cubic chunks tumble past the viewer.

He steps off a falling chunk and tornado-kicks the grey sorcerer.

Snap to extreme wide: the explosion, a skyscraper collapsing, the beast wading through the city; zoom back through the smoke to him.

15–20s

He runs up the fallen mech’s arm and shoulder (worm’s-eye, extreme dutch angle, the mech’s hand huge in the foreground) and jumps from its head as a missile hits the mech behind him; he twists away from the fireball in the air.

A flying hero passes below; he drops onto its back and surfs it for a moment, crouched low with arms out for balance and sabre in hand, skimming low along a burning avenue, the viewer racing alongside past fighting crowds.

He leaps off above an exosuit soldier and brings the sabre down at full speed, foreshortened toward the viewer; hit-stop, impact frame, and the street cracks into cubic fractures on the peak of the phrase.

20–25s

He rolls out and sprints through crossfire between buildings; a grenade explodes ahead and he slides under the blast wave behind an overturned car.

A pulp explorer cracks a whip at him; he catches it on the sabre and pulls the explorer into a beam from the white sorcerer.

Teddy bear warriors charge; he cartwheels across their heads, using them as steps.

Snap to extreme wide: the beast brings its foot down onto the street; fast zoom in to him diving forward out of the impact as the street fractures into flying cubes behind him.

25–30s

A war mech ahead is hit and explodes; he B-twists away from the blast while its severed head tumbles straight toward the viewer, growing in extreme foreshortening on a steep dutch angle.

He side-flips out of its path (second brief slow-motion moment) as it passes under him.

The viewer does not move.

On the final loud note of the soundtrack the head hits the viewer and the image cuts instantly to black.

Silence.

BLADE

His sabre is drawn and in his hand at all times, from the first frame to the last. It is never sheathed or put away.

AUDIO

Ultimate Showdown is the main audio: aggressive neurostep/dubstep with orchestral horns.

Battle sounds mixed underneath: steel clashes, blade whooshes, energy weapons, explosions, collapsing buildings, beast roars.

Stylized action, no gore.

CHARACTER ENTRANCES

Every character who enters the fight is introduced on a dutch angle with extreme foreshortening and wide-angle perspective distortion: their leading weapon, fist, foot or face is huge in the foreground and their body recedes sharply behind.

Each strikes a clear pose and expression while moving, then attacks.

Cyber ninjas: Energy blade thrust straight at the viewer, glowing visor tilted, silent and predatory.
Warrior monks: Low stance, energy sword pointed along its length at the viewer, eyes narrowed, focused.
Helmeted martial artists: Strike a team pose in mid-leap, fists and boots punched toward the viewer, visors angled, then attack in sequence.
Grey sorcerer: Staff end and open hand thrust at the viewer, shouting, eyes crackling with blue lightning.
White sorcerer: Palm pushed toward the viewer, white robes billowing, stern glare, white-gold light gathering.
War mechs: Cannon barrel aimed into the viewer, visor eyes flaring.
Flying hero: Fist-first dive toward the viewer, cape trailing, teeth clenched in a grin.
Exosuit soldier: Gauntlet and rifle huge in the foreground, helmet visor glowing, roaring.
Pulp explorer: Whip hand and uncoiling whip toward the viewer, wry grin, hat brim low.
Teddy bear warriors: Oversized axes raised toward the viewer, small furious faces, teeth bared.
The beast: Seen from far below, open jaws or foot filling the frame, roaring.
Incidental combatants: Screaming faces, weapons foreshortened at the viewer, always mid-charge or mid-swing.
The hero, in contrast: Calm face, slight smile, focused eyes, sabre tip foreshortened toward the viewer.

ATMOSPHERE — CONSTANT, EVERY MOMENT OF THE 30 SECONDS

Tracer fire streaks across the frame and explosions burst in the background in every shot, near and far.

The whole city is burning: fires in every building, tall plumes of black smoke rising into the orange sky, embers drifting through the air.

Lighting is always dramatic side light from the fires and explosions: hard warm light on one side of every character and object, deep dark shadows on the other side, flickering as the fires move.
