# Ships 1: the prompts, for Lati to iterate in Grok Imagine (8 Nov 2026)

Lati, 7 Nov: "earlier ive found that groks imagine on speed setting for space ships gives quick and different results.
Maybe you could parse the prompts you used Id try to iterate with that?" Each prompt = STYLE + one SHIP + one VIEW.
English versions of the Japanese originals (`ships-1.md`), with fixes from Lati's notes folded in.

## STYLE (start every prompt with this)

1980s theatrical sci-fi anime film still, hand-drawn cel animation, dense detailed linework, two-tone cel shading,
hand-painted background art, soft film grain. No logos, no text, no markings on the vessel.

## SHIPS (8 Nov: nothing picked yet; Lati iterates in Grok Imagine, speed setting)

**Flagship (Claude's).** A huge, elegant lead space vessel with a long streamlined hull, off-white and light grey
armour plates with clay-orange (terracotta) accent lines, rows of gun turrets, a low swept-back bridge, large engine
nozzles at the stern. (Visual lead so far: S05.)

**Gemini's ship.** Lati's Grok reference: `media/images/ships-grok/gemini-1.jpg`. A sleek dark gunmetal delta-wing
starfighter-cruiser, a pointed nose with a cockpit canopy, a rainbow stripe running down its spine (red, orange,
yellow, green, blue, violet), long swept wings with spiked wingtip pods, perfectly symmetrical, two round engines side
by side at the rear trailing one broad rainbow exhaust.

**ChatGPT's ship.** A heavy capital space vessel split exactly half white and half black in large clean geometric panels,
perfectly symmetrical, a ring of six engines at the stern whose glowing white exhaust plumes form shock diamonds that
interlace into one symmetrical six-petal rosette of looping light behind the vessel.

**Grok's ship.** A menacing, angular space vessel, almost entirely matte black with thin white accent lines along its edges,
sharp faceted armour, white-glowing engine ports. (Shape lead: S17.)

## VIEWS (one per prompt)

1. Design sheet: the whole vessel seen exactly from the side, on a plain white background.
2. The whole vessel from a three-quarter front angle, stars and a distant nebula behind.
3. Three-quarter rear view, engines firing, stars behind.
4. Top-down plan view of the whole vessel, on a plain grey background.
5. Blazing through warp space at mind-boggling speed: a swirling, rotating tunnel of teal and neon-cyan light, lightning
   arcs striking the hull, speed lines.
6. Seen from directly below its nose, the vessel bearing down on the viewer, overwhelming scale, stars.

## Example, assembled

1980s theatrical sci-fi anime film still, hand-drawn cel animation, dense detailed linework, two-tone cel shading,
hand-painted background art, soft film grain. No logos, no text, no markings on the vessel. A slim, sharp, fast cruiser
with a dark grey hull and rainbow-gradient accent lines (blue, red, yellow, green), blade-like swept wings, perfectly

lightning arcs striking the hull, speed lines.

## Keeping a design once it's picked

Designs drift between generations (Lati, 7 Nov). Once a ship is picked, every later image of it is an edit with the
pick as a reference image (Seedream on ISEKAI ZERO, or Grok's image input), never a fresh text prompt.
