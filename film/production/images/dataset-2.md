## Lip-sync close-ups

A lip-sync model needs the mouth: eye-level camera, the face and mouth unobstructed (no hand, hair or mic across them),
the mic arm ending beside the mouth corner, mouth shapes from closed to a moderate open vowel. Common line on 01-18:
目線の高さのカメラ、顔と口元がはっきり見える、手や髪で口を隠さない、マイクの先は口角の横。Front ones add:
前髪は眉の上で一直線に切り揃え、分け目なし。
