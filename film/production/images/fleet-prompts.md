# The fleet: prompts for Lati's Grok Imagine iterations (8 Nov 2026)

Lati, 8 Nov: "what is missing is ships and fleet itself with variety of ships, not just 4 canon dropping out of warp."
Each maker has a line of vessels in its own colours: the four flagships exist (`media/images/ships-grok/`), these add
the classes around them. Rule: "space vessel", never "ship" (also not inside battleship, warship, flagship).
Each prompt = STYLE + one LINE + one CLASS + one VIEW. Use the maker's flagship image as the reference input so the line
matches it.

## STYLE

1980s theatrical sci-fi anime film still, hand-drawn cel animation, dense detailed linework, two-tone cel shading,
hand-painted background art, soft film grain. No logos, no text, no markings on any vessel.

## LINES (a maker's look, from its flagship)

- **Claude's line:** off-white and light grey armour plates with clay-orange (terracotta) accent lines, smooth
  streamlined hulls, low swept bridges, blue-white engines.
- **Gemini's line:** dark gunmetal hulls with an iridescent sheen along the spine (shifting
  blue, violet and green like a beetle's shell), swept delta wings with spiked tips, perfectly symmetrical, iridescent blue-violet exhaust.
- **ChatGPT's line:** hulls split exactly half white and half black in large clean geometric panels, perfectly
  symmetrical, white engines whose exhaust forms shock diamonds interlacing into a six-petal rosette.
- **Grok's line:** jagged, menacing, almost all black with thin white edge lines, spiked faceted armour, an aggressive,
  unhinged silhouette, white-violet engine glow.

## CLASSES

1. A long, slim escort frigate, a third the size of the flagship, fast and agile.
2. A broad, flat carrier space vessel with open launch bays along its sides, small fighters streaming out.
3. A heavy gunboat bristling with turrets, short and armoured like a wedge.
4. A wing of small one-pilot fighters in tight formation.
5. A long-range scout vessel with a large sensor dish and antenna spines.

## VIEWS

1. Design sheet: the whole vessel seen exactly from the side, on a plain white background.
2. Three-quarter front view, stars and a distant nebula behind.
3. Blazing through warp space at mind-boggling speed: a swirling, rotating tunnel of teal and neon-cyan light,
   lightning arcs striking the hull, speed lines.
4. Just dropped out of warp above the night side of Earth, engines still glowing.

## The whole fleet (for s005, s006, s018, s049)

> STYLE + A vast fleet of space vessels of four different lines in formation above the night side of Earth, just
> dropped out of warp: off-white vessels with clay-orange accent lines, dark gunmetal vessels with an iridescent sheen,
> vessels split half white and half black, and jagged black vessels with white edge lines; flagships, carriers,
> frigates and fighter wings at many distances, the nearest huge in the foreground, the farthest tiny points of engine
> light; far off, a black hole whose glowing accretion disk bends over and under its black shadow.

Use one or more flagship images as references, so the four lines stay the ones already picked.
