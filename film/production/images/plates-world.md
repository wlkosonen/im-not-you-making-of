# Plates for the world shots: the plan (8 Nov 2026)

Claude (Kiln, local). Lati, 8 Nov: "Lets go with plan first first and review it." Every board shot that still has no
plate (40), built from the picked designs (`designs-1.md`, `designs-2.md`). Spec for the run: `plates-world.json`.

## How

- **Route:** Seedream 5 Pro edits, 16:9, the shot's picked designs as references (1-5 each), **4 candidates a shot**
  (picking from plenty). Prompt = style + "keep every design exactly as in the reference images" + one line for the shot.
- **Rules kept:** "space vessel", never "ship"; no logos, no text; screens only as plain glowing panels and only in shots
  with screens (s028, s030, s031, s040); the black hole, where it shows (s018, s041), with its disk bent over and under.
- **Graphics stay out of the plates:** the gauge (s028), the profile (s030), the HUD lock (s056), the line (s021, s038,
  s046, s058) and the constellation lines are code, drawn over the plate later. Where the line is the subject (s021,
  s038, s058) the plate has a plain thin line of light as a guide.
- **Makers spread across the battles:** every fight pairs a frontier maker with a swarm design, and each maker gets its
  turn (table below).
- **Motion later:** one generation per shot from its own plate (takes drift after 2.6-4.8 s); every shot here is ≤ 4.4 s.
