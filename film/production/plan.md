the design must do. The research comes before both, because the verses are written from it.

## Release from day one

Lati, film 2: "Last time release decisions and forum posts got added there too, id like to keep it clean this time
around." So `production/release/` holds all of that and the export leaves it out. This repo stays internal; the public
record is a clean copy built at release by `export_public.py`, film 2's tool.

The description, README and pinned comment stay separate, and the description stays simple. Here, though, the full source
list must be public: where it goes without pushing the video into tutorials is a release question to settle early.

Film 2's numbers to 5 Nov (`~/checked-me/production/release/log.md`): the film 1 fix worked. "Official Music Video" in
the title, a story-first description, genre tags (ai, slopcore, doompop), tools lower. After YouTube's broad test it
landed as a suggestion beside the other slopcore videos and CTR climbed (Lati, 6 Nov), then the home feed took it
(1,419 of 1,575 views on 5 Nov). Keep that shape here; the source list is the new risk to it.
